1Design language: SIGNAL — instrumentation, not terminals

The premise: every dev-cyberpunk site reaches for neon signage and a fake shell. This one reaches for scientific instruments — oscilloscopes, spectrum analysers, Esper-style optical readouts. Deep ink field, hairline geometry, real measured values, one accent lit at a time. Restrained 95% of the time so the 5% that moves actually lands. Pick per-dimension below and I'll consolidate into one spec.

1aFOUNDATION — palette · type · materials · motion rules
01 / FIELD + ACCENTS
INK 05070D
PANEL 0A0E18
RAIL 0D1220
SIG CYAN
ERR MAGENTA
WARN AMBER

Accents share lightness + chroma in oklch, vary only in hue — so nothing screams louder than its neighbour. One accent per screen: cyan for the CV, amber for photos, magenta reserved for live/error states. Glow is always a shadow of the element's own colour, never a white halo.

02 / TYPE
Space Grotesk
DISPLAY / UI — 700, tracking −3%, sizes 13 · 20 · 32 · 64 · 128
JetBrains Mono
ANNOTATION / DATA / LABELS — uppercase, tracking +6–22%
Newsreader — long-form prose for the blog, because reading 1,200 words of monospace is a punishment.
BLOG BODY — 300, 19px, 72ch measure
03 / MATERIALS
MEASURE GRID 22PX
SCANLINE 1:3
BLOOM FROM BELOW
SWEEP PASS 5.5S

Corners are cut, not rounded (2px radius max). Borders are 1px hairlines at 13% opacity. Depth comes from light, not shadow.

04 / MOTION LAWS
iInstruments idle, they don't dance. Ambient motion is slow (4–8s), low-contrast, and loops without a beat you can tap to.
iiInteraction is fast and mechanical. 120–220ms, cubic-bezier(.2,.8,.2,1). Hover lights an edge; it does not bounce.
iiiReveals are apertures and sweeps — clip-path and scan passes, never fades-up-with-blur.
ivNothing flashes, nothing loops under 400ms, nothing autoplays sound. prefers-reduced-motion kills all ambient layers.
