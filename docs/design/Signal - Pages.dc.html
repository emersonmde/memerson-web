5PAGE MOCKUPS — two art directions, three fixes, one lightbox, one transition

Fixes carried in from turn 4. The elbow no longer appears from nowhere — the tube is now the sign's own underline: it runs the width of the name, bends at the left through a real radiused corner, and drops into the rail, so the charge visibly leaves the signage and enters the index. Rail colour is no longer a guess: each plate owns a rail segment whose gradient starts at that plate's hue and ends at the next one's, so dot, offshoot and brackets are the same colour as the rail by construction, not by approximation. Scroll effects are now driven by a real scroll handler instead of CSS scroll-timelines (which silently did nothing inside a nested scroller) — starfield parallax, headings resolving out of blur, segments charging as they arrive, sticky section numbers, footer floor tilt. Lightbox reverts to the 2e borrowed-light treatment and the only transition left is Redraw.

Two directions: 5a is the clean cut refined. 5b is the same page taken to Night City — rain on glass, raked blinds, concrete grime, hazard tape, sodium and acid yellow instead of cyan, hot neon against filthy surfaces.
